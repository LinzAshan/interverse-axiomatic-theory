A framework for cross-universe power analysis

Evidence decides. Not popularity.

Interverse Axiomatic Theory reasons through fictional power and ability matchups the way a rigorous debate should — by testing what's actually demonstrated, not what's loudest.

The Method

Four questions, asked in order, every time.

01

Profile

Every character is understood fully on their own terms before anything is compared.

02

Compatibility

Two universes rarely share the same rules. We check whether an ability can even reach its target before assuming it does.

03

Mechanism

Abilities are judged by what they actually do, never by their name or how they look on screen.

04

Verdict

The conclusion states what the evidence currently supports — a lean, never a guarantee.

What this isn't

No popularity contests. No forced winners.

  • Not a popularity contest. Fan consensus and hype don't count as evidence.
  • Not a guaranteed answer. When the evidence is genuinely insufficient, the verdict says so.
  • Not narrative-driven. What a story probably wants to happen next is a separate question from what the demonstrated mechanics support.
  • Not static. New canon changes the conclusion. It never changes the method.

Interverse Axiomatic Theory is an ongoing, evidence-first project for analyzing fictional power systems across universes.
